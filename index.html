Biology tells us how the body works. Art helps us see it differently.

We’re a high school club bringing anatomy, physiology, and illustration together. Every two weeks, we explore a body part through a new creative theme, then share the science behind the sketch.

Anatomy, reimagined.

One body part. An unexpected theme. A new way to understand what’s inside us.

Showing all 3 studies

Brain × Beehive

A hive of ideas.

The brain processes sensory information, coordinates movement, and supports memory, emotion, and thought.

The science behind the sketch +

Neurons communicate through electrical and chemical signals. Networks of these cells help us think, feel, and respond to the world.

The honeycomb is an artistic metaphor for many connected parts working together.

Heart × Botanical garden

Life in every beat.

The heart pumps blood through the lungs and the rest of the body, helping deliver oxygen and nutrients.

The science behind the sketch +

The right side of the heart sends blood to the lungs. The left side sends oxygen-rich blood around the body.

Branching stems echo blood vessels, while blooming flowers represent the life this circulation supports.

Lungs × Forest

The forest within.

The lungs bring oxygen into the blood and remove carbon dioxide when you breathe.

The science behind the sketch +

Air travels through branching airways to tiny sacs called alveoli. Oxygen and carbon dioxide move between the air and blood there.

The branching trees echo the shape of the airways. Leaves are an artistic theme, rather than lung anatomy.

Find your part.

Artists, science enthusiasts, builders, storytellers. There’s a place for your curiosity here.

Draw.Illustration+

Turn a body part and a creative theme into an illustration. Try pencil, paint, digital art, or a medium you love. Each drawing project runs for two weeks.

Discover.Body-part descriptions+

Research what each body part does and help write the science that accompanies the art. Make anatomy and physiology easy for other students to understand.

Build.Website+

Bring our illustrations and explanations together in the club’s online portfolio. Help shape how visitors explore the artwork and learn about the body.

Share.Social media & marketing+

Help our artwork reach more people through Instagram. Plan posts, share drawing progress, and introduce the science behind each finished piece.
